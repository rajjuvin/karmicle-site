Karmicle Privacy Policy

Your Life. Your Karma. Chronicled.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version. Karmicle keeps your milestones, photos and settings on your phone. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and it makes no network connections. We, the maker of Karmicle, never receive your data and could not read it if we wanted to.

What Karmicle stores, and where

The milestones you write (titles, notes, categories, dates, impact), the photos you attach, and your settings are saved in a private database and private folder inside the app on your phone. They are not uploaded anywhere. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it, unless you made a backup file yourself.

What Karmicle does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics.
  • It shows no ads and contains no advertising or tracking software.
  • It does not sell, share or send your information to anyone.
  • It does not use an AI service. The life summary is written on your phone from your own entries by fixed templates.

Permissions, and why Karmicle asks for each

Karmicle asks only when you first use the feature. You can say no, and the rest of the app keeps working.

  • Photos. Only if you attach a photo to a milestone. You pick the photos in your phone's own picker. Karmicle receives only the ones you choose, makes a smaller copy and keeps it inside the app. It does not browse the rest of your library.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. Only if you tap the microphone to add a milestone by speaking. Karmicle listens only while you are using that button, and it only uses speech recognition that runs on your phone. If your phone cannot recognise speech on the device itself, Karmicle turns voice entry off rather than send your voice to a server. No audio is kept.
  • Face ID, fingerprint or passcode. Only if you turn on App lock. Your phone does the checking and tells Karmicle only "yes" or "no". Karmicle never sees or stores your face, fingerprint or passcode.
  • Notifications. Only if you turn on the weekly reminder. The reminder is scheduled on your phone. No notification service or server is involved.

Things you choose to do

  • Sharing or exporting. When you export a backup, a summary image, or a text file, Karmicle hands the file to your phone's share sheet. You decide where it goes. After that, it is outside Karmicle's control and this policy. Text exports leave out your private notes unless you switch them on.
  • Your phone's own backup. iCloud (iPhone) or Google backup (Android) may copy app data as part of backing up your whole phone. That is controlled by your phone's settings and the phone maker's terms, not by Karmicle.

Children

Karmicle is a general-purpose personal journal. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your control

Because the data is only on your phone, you are in full control. You can edit or delete any milestone, delete photos, make a backup, or remove everything by deleting the app. There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

Changes to this policy

If Karmicle ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated first and the date above will change. A change that sent any data off your phone would need your clear permission in the app.
