Karmicle

Your Life. Your Karma. Chronicled.

Karmicle is a calm, private place to record the milestones of your life: the gains you are proud of and the pains that taught you something. See them as a timeline, as simple insights, and as a journey path, and have a life summary written for you, all on your phone.

  • Add a milestone in a few taps, by typing or by speaking, with photos if you like.
  • Choose Personal, Professional, or your whole Life, and any range of years.
  • Export a summary as an image or a text file whenever you decide to.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Nothing leaves your phone.
