Your Life. Chronicled.

Get in touch. Email karmicle.support@gmail.com. Please do not send your milestones or photos. Describe what happened and which phone and version you use (Settings, at the bottom under About, shows the version).

Back up your milestones

Karmicle keeps everything on your phone and has no cloud copy, so a lost or reset phone means lost milestones unless you made a backup. Open Settings, tap Export backup, and save the file somewhere you trust, such as your own cloud drive or computer. To restore on a new phone, install Karmicle, open Settings, tap Import backup, and choose the file. Importing replaces what is in the app, and Karmicle checks the whole file first so a damaged file changes nothing.

App lock

Turn it on in Settings. Karmicle then asks for Face ID, your fingerprint, or your phone passcode when you open the app. If you cannot unlock, use "Use passcode". If you forget your phone passcode, that is a matter for your phone's maker, and Karmicle cannot reset it.

Voice entry

Tap the microphone when adding a milestone and say, for example, "Gain, ran my first half marathon, health, last September". You always check the result before it is saved. Voice entry works only when your phone can recognise speech on the device itself, in English. If it is not available, Karmicle tells you, and typing always works. Check Settings on your phone to be sure Karmicle is allowed to use the microphone and speech recognition.

Weekly reminder

Turn it on in Settings and choose a day and time. If no reminder arrives, check that notifications are allowed for Karmicle in your phone's settings, and that battery saving is not stopping the app's alerts.

Photos

You can attach up to six photos to a milestone. Karmicle keeps its own smaller copy inside the app, so deleting the original from your gallery does not remove it from Karmicle. Open a photo on the milestone's page, tap Delete photo, and tap again to confirm.
